"How Solo Travel Helped me Become a More Confident Leader"
Solo travel changed my life in ways I never expected. I didn’t set out to “find myself” — I simply wanted to see the world. But along the way, I found clarity, confidence, and a stronger sense of leadership than I had ever experienced before.

1. I learned that fear disappears when action begins
Standing in a foreign airport alone teaches you very quickly that courage grows with movement. Every decision built confidence.

2. I became a problem-solver
Travel tests you: missed trains, unexpected situations, cultural differences.
But each challenge sharpened my mind and made me a stronger decision-maker.

3. I found my inner voice
When you’re on your own, you rely on your intuition.
Solo travel strengthened my trust in myself — a core leadership skill.

4. Leadership is confidence — and travel builds confidence
Today, I apply those lessons in my career as an EA.
Travel didn’t just show me the world — it showed me who I am.
